# 📊 HR Analytics: Employee Attrition & Retention Dashboard

An interactive executive dashboard built in Power BI to analyze workforce dynamics, key attrition drivers, and employee turnover patterns using the IBM HR Analytics dataset from Kaggle for Syntecxhub internship - Task 3.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview
Employee attrition significantly impacts organizational productivity, workplace morale, and operational costs. This project leverages exploratory data analysis and dynamic visual reporting to uncover core drivers of employee turnover—such as monthly income, job role satisfaction, department structures, and demographic trends.

The goal of this dashboard is to provide HR leaders and decision-makers with clear, actionable insights to improve talent retention strategies.

---

## 📈 Key Dashboard Metrics & KPIs
* **Total Employees:** Overall workforce headcount tracked across company records.
* **Active Employees:** Count of currently retained staff.
* **Attrition Count & Rate:** Total employee departures and the corresponding turnover percentage.
* **Average Age & Tenure:** Demographic profiles and average years spent at the organization.
* **Average Monthly Income:** Salary benchmarks across departments and job roles.

---

## 📊 Visualizations & Analytical Insights
* **Attrition by Education Field (Donut Chart):** Breaks down departure proportions across different educational backgrounds.
* **Attrition by Department & Job Role (Bar Charts):** Pinpoints specific business units and job functions experiencing elevated attrition rates.
* **Demographic Breakdown:** Illustrates turnover distribution across age groups, job satisfaction levels, and compensation tiers.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
* **Power BI Desktop:** Report layout design, visual configuration, and dynamic cross-filtering.
* **Power Query Editor:** Data cleaning, type transformations, and schema preparation.
* **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions):** Data aggregation and metric modeling.
* **GitHub:** Portfolio presentation and version control.
